Know next month's sales before it starts

Add your daily sales history and SalesCast projects the days ahead, with a likely range for each day and a check of how accurate the method is on your own numbers.

Two years of generated daily sales with weekly and yearly patterns, so you can see how the forecast works before adding your own numbers.

How it works

Learns your patterns

It fits the overall trend, the day-of-week rhythm (weekends versus weekdays) and, with a year or more of history, the seasonal curve.

Tests itself first

The last stretch of your history is hidden, predicted, and compared with what really sold. That gives you the average error shown with each forecast.

Shows the range

Every forecast day comes with a low and high value that actual sales should land between about four times out of five.

Questions

What file format do I need?

A CSV or pasted text with a date in the first column and sales in the second. A header row is fine. Several rows on the same day are added together, and missing days are filled in from the days around them.

How much history is needed?

At least 28 days. Four to eight weeks gives a usable weekly pattern, and a year or more lets it learn seasonality.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculations run in your browser, and nothing you add leaves this page.

Does it know about promotions or holidays?

Not yet. It sees only dates and sales, so a planned promotion or holiday spike will not appear in the forecast.

SalesCast forecasts are estimates based on past patterns. Treat them as a planning guide.
